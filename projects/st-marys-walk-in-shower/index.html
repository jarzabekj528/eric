From an older tub to a low-step shower

This St. Marys bathroom project replaced an almond tub-shower unit with a low-step walk-in shower. The existing project photographs show a grab bar, sliding glass doors, wood-look plank flooring, and an updated gray vanity.

Details to discuss for your bathroom

The lower shower entry and grab bar are useful reference points when planning easier access. Your room may need a different base, door configuration, or fixture placement. The estimate visit is the time to compare those choices with your existing dimensions.
