Tile Installation in Lima, OH

Bring coordinated tile finishes to a bathroom or kitchen renovation. Discuss shower walls, floors, and backsplash work with Say When Contracting in Lima, Ohio.

Request a Free Estimate

Match tile to its purpose

Wall tile, shower tile, and floor tile serve different needs. Consider cleaning, grout joints, surface texture, and the scale of the pattern within the room. Confirm that the product you choose is suitable for its intended location before buying or scheduling installation.

The base matters as much as the tile

Tile installation starts with a suitable surface. Shower areas need a planned waterproofing system beneath the visible finish; tile and grout alone are not a substitute. Existing damage, unevenness, and the condition of the backing can change the preparation included in your estimate.

Plan edges, patterns, and fixtures together

Layout decisions are easiest before installation. Discuss where cuts will fall, how corners and exposed edges will finish, and how the pattern meets outlets or plumbing fixtures. Bring sample tiles if you have them, including information about size and finish.

Coordinate tile with the remodeling schedule

A shower wall, kitchen backsplash, and bathroom floor each fit into the project at a different stage. Cabinet and countertop placement may need to be settled first. Ask when the surface can be cleaned or used and which care instructions apply to your selected materials.

Questions about tile installation

Can you discuss a kitchen backsplash with my remodel?
Yes. Bring backsplash ideas to the kitchen consultation so tile, outlets, cabinets, and countertops can be planned together. Confirm the final installation scope in the estimate.
Is grout enough to waterproof a shower?
No. A shower needs an appropriate waterproofing system behind the finish surface. Ask how the substrate, seams, and transitions will be prepared.
Can I supply my own tile?
Ask before purchasing. Quantity, suitability, matching lots, edge pieces, and delivery timing need to be confirmed so the materials work with the installation plan.
