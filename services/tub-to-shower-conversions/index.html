Tub-to-Shower Conversions in Lima, OH

Replace an underused bathtub with a shower designed around your space. Say When Contracting offers tub-to-shower conversion consultations in Lima and nearby Ohio communities.

Request a Free Estimate

Make better use of an existing tub area

If you rarely use your bathtub or find its high side inconvenient, a conversion may make the room more practical. Start by discussing who uses the bathroom, whether another bathtub will remain in the home, and what you want from the new shower.

What changes during a conversion?

The scope can include removing the tub, preparing the opening, addressing the drain and valve, installing a shower base and wall surfaces, and fitting an enclosure. The condition behind the old tub matters. Any concealed damage or required preparation should be discussed as part of the work plan.

Compare the shower options before ordering

Base dimensions, threshold height, door movement, storage, and fixture reach all affect daily use. A sliding door and a hinged door need different clearances. If you want a seat or grab bars, raise those preferences early so the wall support and layout can be considered.

A conversion has its own schedule

A tub replacement does not automatically follow a one-day installation promise. Material availability, preparation, plumbing changes, and the selected finish system affect timing. Ask Eric for the expected period without shower access and the steps that must be complete before use.

Questions about tub-to-shower conversions

Will the shower fit where the tub was?
Many conversions use the existing tub opening, but measurements, drainage, and the chosen base determine the final fit. A home visit is needed before confirming the layout.
Should I remove the only bathtub in my home?
Consider your household routines and whether anyone needs a bath. Discuss those needs before committing; a shower conversion is a practical choice only when it fits the way you use the home.
What affects tub-to-shower conversion cost?
Removal, the condition of the opening, plumbing work, shower materials, doors, and accessories all affect the price. Request a written estimate for your room rather than relying on a generic advertised package.
