Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Say When Contracting respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this estimate and appointment website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect the project type, project address, city, ZIP code, name, phone number, and any email address you choose to provide. If you schedule an in-home visit, we also collect the appointment date and time.

We also collect limited technical and advertising information, such as the referring page, campaign parameters, click identifiers, browser or device information, and page or conversion events.

How we use information

  • To respond to your remodeling request and provide an estimate.
  • To schedule, confirm, and manage an in-home visit.
  • To call or text you about the request when you have given consent.
  • To understand advertising performance, prevent abuse, and improve the website.
  • To maintain business records and comply with applicable obligations.

Calls and text messages

When you affirmatively select the consent checkbox on the estimate form, you agree that Say When Contracting may call or text the phone number you provide about your remodeling request, including through automated technology. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to a text message to opt out or contact us using the details below.

Advertising and cookies

This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure page visits, accepted leads, and completed appointment requests and to help us understand advertising performance. Meta and other service providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technology to receive information about activity on this website and elsewhere for measurement and advertising.

You can manage Meta advertising preferences in Meta Ad Preferences, use your browser's cookie controls, or visit the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate this website and process requests, including Cloudflare for hosting and security checks, GoHighLevel for customer and appointment management, and Meta for advertising measurement. These providers process information for their respective services and under their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and protection

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal or operational requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you. Some records may be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.
